What you can do

Everything for your application, organized into three stages.

From first search to final offer, each stage of Study Compass is built around what you actually need to decide next.

Stage 1

Discover

Search universities by country, budget, and course. Filter scholarships by eligibility and deadline. Estimate real costs with live currency conversion.

Stage 2

Prepare

Download SOP and recommendation templates, build a document checklist, upload files, and track every application in one dashboard.

Stage 3

Decide

Get financial aid advice, ask questions in the community forum, and follow visa guidance until your offer becomes a plane ticket.
